What is Ignition?

One platform, every question answered

Is it a SCADA? Does it work with IIoT? Is it a MES system? Does it provide HMI and alarming for your plant floor operators? Can you build automatically generated custom reports and save them as PDFs? Can you connect Edge systems to your entire enterprise? Can you use Ignition as a mobile platform?

The answer is yes to all of the above.

Ignition is more than a product — it's an Industry 4.0 platform designed to bridge the gap between the plant floor and the executive floor. Think of the product as the app that runs on your phone, and the platform as the operating system itself. Once a truly universal platform is in place, the products are only limited by imagination.

If a function your company needs isn't already covered by the extensive list of modules, a solution may exist on the Ignition Exchange, in the Designer's built-in Python scripting tools, or — if you need to go further still — the SDK. Ignition is a platform that grows into the future, each component building on the others as time and needs permit.
